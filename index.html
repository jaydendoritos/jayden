THE SHORT VERSION

I’m Jayden. Online, I go by w1vvo. I started out building on Roblox, then got pulled into pretty much every side of making a game.

These days I split my time between scripting, modeling, building, and finding better ways to work with AI. I’ve also managed projects and communities, worked on marketing and acquisitions, and helped with business and legal issues along the way.

I like figuring out what a project needs next—whether that’s a better workflow, a clearer plan, or someone to sort out the details.
